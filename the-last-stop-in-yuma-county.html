Film review · 2023 · Neo-noir / Thriller

The Last Stop in
Yuma County.

Directed by Francis Galluppi★★★★½ Laura's rating

A wickedly controlled pressure-cooker that turns one empty desert diner into a sweat-soaked nightmare. Funny, mean and genuinely unpredictable.

Set in a lonely desert stopover, this is the kind of film that understands the power of a confined space and a tightening screw. Every new arrival changes the temperature, every silence feels deliberate, and the tension never lets up.

It is lean, nasty and darkly entertaining — a thriller that knows exactly when to hold back and when to strike. A perfect late-night watch for anyone who likes their suspense sun-baked, cynical and just a little unhinged.
